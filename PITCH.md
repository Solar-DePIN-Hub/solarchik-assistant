# Pitch — Solarchik Assistant

Solarchik Assistant is a pocket AI on your phone, not another chat box.

Sol lives on the home screen. You hold one big mic button. He answers in your language, short and out loud.

When you cannot take a call, the AI secretary picks up on a real line, talks to the caller, and leaves you a short summary: who, why, what to do next. Callbacks show up as follow-ups on the home screen.

An agent wallet on Solana (devnet for the hackathon) does on-chain steps only after you tap confirm. A daily check-in keeps the habit. For Seeker owners, a small Season plan suggests one real thing a day and shows your SKR balance, with no auto-signing and no grinding. The roof run stays as a small bonus, not the product.

Same friend. Same voice. Talk, handle the call, keep the on-chain habit.
