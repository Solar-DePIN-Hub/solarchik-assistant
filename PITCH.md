# Sol: AI secretary for Seeker

**Problem.** I miss calls in meetings. Some are clients, some are friends asking to be paid back, some are scams. Voicemail doesn't help, and the call and the wallet live in different apps, so things get forgotten.

**Solution.** Sol picks up when I can't. An AI secretary talks to the caller on a real phone line, writes me a short note, and turns the call into cards: call back, remind, pay. Every morning the cards wait in one stack on Today, with my Seeker Season tasks and habits. One swipe per card, then I clock in and keep my streak.

**Money.** Circle is the people I save myself. Send and Request work through Solana Pay links I share by SMS, and the app shows "Paid ✓" when the money lands. Every payment is approved in my own wallet over Mobile Wallet Adapter. The app never holds a key.

**Why Solana.** A 0.01 SOL payment from a call settled in about 3 seconds for a fee of 0.00008 SOL. Paying back a $1 lunch only makes sense when the fee is a fraction of a cent. Seeker owners already have a wallet on the phone, so they're my first users.

**Business.** Free to start. Pro at $7.99 a month (proposed) for your own secretary number. A Business plan for small service businesses that miss calls while they work. A small fee on Circle payments later.

**Traction.** Live on Solana mainnet, tested by 4 people, my family and friends.

**Team.** Vadym Bilobrovets, solo founder, Ukraine. I design and build the Android app and the backend myself.

> I didn't pick up once. Sol did.
