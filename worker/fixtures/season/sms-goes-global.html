SMS goes global: Solana Mobile Stack opens to every Android OEM and Seeker Season 2 Forges On

Jul 14, 2026

At MWC 2026, we introduced the Solana Mobile Stack (SMS) for Android OEMs. The Seed Vault, Seeker Wallet, and dApp Store you use every day — now available as a tech stack for any Android phone manufacturer.

‍We built it for Seeker first. Shipped 200K+ devices, saw $5B+ in onchain volume, and 500+ dApps thanks to your support.
‍Now it scales with SMS. The ecosystem you helped build is about to get a lot bigger.

‍Your Seeker can be an AI agent now. Yes, you read that right. 



SeekerClaw dropped in the dApp Store on February 27th and immediately became one of the most interesting things you can install on your phone.

It’s an open-source AI agent that lives on your Seeker 24/7. It can monitor your wallet, swap on Jupiter, control your camera, launch apps, and talk to you through Telegram. 
You can download SeekerClaw on the Solana dApp Store now.

‍The MONOLITH hackathon is a wrap: 393 new apps just entered the arena.



The first Solana Mobile Hackathon gave us Gib Meme, Amp Pay, Mattle, and Nomadz — apps still beloved today. MONOLITH is bigger across the board.

The MONOLITH Hackathon by RadiantsDAO closed on March 9th. We saw 888 devs and 393 app submissions, nearly 2x the number of submissions for the inaugural Solana Mobile hackathon. 

Now, judges are in the lab to decide who will take home the lion’s share of $125k in prizes, plus the special SKR integration prize. 
Winners announcement coming soon.

Seeker Season 2 forges on, featuring new drops and new perks.

And of course, Seeker Season 2 continues to delight with new app experiences and Seeker exclusives, including: 

↳ Flash Trade — The first 1,000 Seekers get $10 to start trading perps on Flash. What are you waiting for?

↳ Colony — The first idle resource-trading strategy game on Solana and the first game to integrate staked SKR directly into gameplay is now live on Seeker. Rewards scale with your staked balance, plus Seeker exclusives. 

↳ Faenora — Get exclusive in-game perks and a MCHY airdrop when you buy a mercenary with SKR. Make sure to play in landscape mode for the best game experience.

↳ ORO — Buy, sell, and stake tokenized gold on Oro to climb the leaderboard tiers and get exclusive rewards. A chunk of the entire S1 nugget pool is reserved just for Seeker users, plus boosted multipliers for Seekers.
All available now on the Solana dApp Store.
