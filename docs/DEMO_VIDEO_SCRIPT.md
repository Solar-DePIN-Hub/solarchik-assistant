# Solarchik Assistant 1.1.1: 2:30 demo video (shot list)

You record this on your own Android phone with the built-in screen recorder (sound on, "media + mic").
Language: **English** (the app's default). Times are targets; the full take is about 2:30.

## Pre-flight (the evening before or the morning of 10 Oct, before 12:00 Kyiv)

1. **Install** `solarchik-assistant.apk` from the v1.1.1 release:
   https://github.com/Solar-DePIN-Hub/solarchik-assistant/releases/tag/v1.1.1
   (Allow "install unknown apps" for the browser. sha256 is in the release notes and README.)
2. Open the app once and finish onboarding. Allow **notifications** and the **microphone**.
3. **Wallet:** Phantom, Solflare or Seed Vault on **mainnet** with about **0.05 SOL** (fees are ~0.000005 SOL; nothing in
   the demo spends more than that). Today → **Set up wallet** → approve in the wallet. Check that Today shows your SOL balance.
4. **Settings → Language:** English (Ukrainian is the second option if you want a Ukrainian take later).
5. **Secretary:** Today → **Try a call** once as a rehearsal: it dials the demo line **+380 91 481 0885**, Sol answers.
   Say a sentence, hang up, and check the note appears in **Calls** within ~30 s. (Your carrier may charge for the call.)
6. **Don't sign the check-in while rehearsing.** It's one per day (the day resets at 03:00 Kyiv = 00:00 UTC), and the video
   needs it. No run in Play is needed: the check-in is one tap.
7. **Season rules:** Agents → Season Agent → "Official Season rules" should already show "Summer Wrapped…". The server
   only reads the sources again if the last check was more than 5 minutes ago; otherwise it shows the latest result.
   Both are fine on camera.
8. **Morning briefing:** Today → briefing card → set the time (08:30 is the default). Before recording, after the test call, tap
   **Play briefing** once so you know the voice works (volume up, not silent mode).
9. Phone: Do Not Disturb **on** (but allow calls), battery > 50%, brightness up, Wi-Fi or a good LTE signal,
   close other apps. Hide your seed phrase and any private notifications.

## If something goes wrong on camera

- Sol doesn't answer: check the connection and ask again; the typed box works too.
- The secretary note is slow: cut, and come back to **Calls** after 30 s.
- The wallet doesn't open: the wallet app must be installed and on mainnet. Tap the check-in again.
- "Checked a few minutes ago" toast on the rules check is normal (5-minute server throttle).
- Never show the seed phrase, and never send real funds to the address a caller dictates.
